Amazon KDP Simplified

Publish Your Own Book on Amazon and Earn in Dollars, Even If You Have Never Written Anything Before.

👨‍🏫 Instructor: Thokerunga Innocent 📹 10 Video Lessons ⚡ Includes Book Formatter Pro 💬 WhatsApp Mentorship
Full Module Breakdown

The 10 Practical Lessons

A step-by-step masterclass teaching the exact process from market research to royalties.

Lesson 01 ⏱️ 15–25 mins

Amazon KDP From Zero: How the Business Works

Understand what Amazon KDP is, how royalties work, what types of books beginners can create, and the complete process from idea to published book.

Topics Covered: How books are sold • eBook vs. paperback differences • Royalty split (70% vs 35% vs 60% print) • Beginner genres (Cookbooks, self-help, how-to, planners, journals, puzzle books, faith books) • The full 8-stage workflow • Goal setting: committing to publish book #1.
Lesson 02 ⏱️ 20–30 mins

Set Up Your Amazon KDP Account and Get Paid in Uganda

Learn how to set up your KDP account, complete the required information, handle tax details, and set up payment information correctly.

Topics Covered: Clean account registration • Identity verification guidelines • Tax interview walk-through with African TIN • Setting up USD virtual bank accounts (Payoneer / Wise) to receive direct payments • Common beginner mistakes • Keeping your account 100% compliant with Amazon policies.
Lesson 03 ⏱️ 25–35 mins

KDP Market Research: Find Book Ideas People Already Buy

Learn how to research Amazon, study existing books, find demand, check competition, and choose topics with a real market.

Topics Covered: Amazon category browsing • Estimating book sales from Best Sellers Rank (BSR) • Analyzing competitor reviews to find content gaps • Evaluating pricing, cover quality, and publication age • The 5-Point Idea Scoring System: Demand, Competition, Buyer Intent, Content Ability, Profit Potential.
Lesson 04 ⏱️ 20–30 mins

KDP Keyword Research: Find Search Terms Buyers Use

Learn how to find useful search terms, understand Amazon autocomplete, study competing books, and build a strong keyword list.

Topics Covered: Amazon incognito autocomplete suggestions • Competitor title/subtitle keyword teardown • The 7 KDP backend keyword slots • Long-tail keywords vs broad keywords • How to avoid policy-violating keyword stuffing.
Lesson 05 ⏱️ 30–45 mins

Create Your Book With AI: From Idea to Complete Draft

Learn how to build an outline, create chapters with AI, improve the writing, fact-check and edit the manuscript, and prepare it for formatting.

Topics Covered: Designing comprehensive Tables of Contents • Prompting AI chapter-by-chapter • Eliminating repetitive robotic text • Fact-checking and editing guidelines • Adding author value and structuring clean MS Word documents.
Lesson 06 ⏱️ 20–30 mins

Create a Professional KDP Cover With AI

Learn how to plan a cover, choose fonts and colors, create images with AI, and prepare a cover that fits Amazon's technical requirements.

Topics Covered: Calculating trim sizes, page counts, and spine widths • Front cover vs full wrap cover layout • AI artwork generation • Typography hierarchy and thumbnail readability • Exporting print-ready PDF covers.
Lesson 07 ⏱️ 20–30 mins

Format Your Book With Book Formatter Pro

Learn how to turn a raw manuscript into a clean, professional KDP interior using Book Formatter Pro.

Topics Covered: Opening manuscripts • Setting 6x9, 5.5x8.5, or custom trim sizes • Automated margins, gutters, and page breaks • Chapter headers, alternating page numbers, and table of contents generation • Exporting Amazon-compliant print PDFs in minutes.
Lesson 08 ⏱️ 25–40 mins

Publish Your eBook and Paperback on Amazon KDP

Walk through the KDP publishing process, book details, manuscript upload, cover upload, preview, pricing, territories, and publishing.

Topics Covered: Entering title, subtitle, author name, and HTML book description • Category selection and keyword entry • Manuscript upload and Print Previewer inspection • Pricing strategy, territories, and hitting "Publish".
Lesson 09 ⏱️ 20–30 mins

Get Your First KDP Sales: Reviews, Promotion and Amazon Ads

Learn simple ways to market a new book, encourage legitimate reviews, use basic promotion methods, and understand when Amazon Ads may make sense.

Topics Covered: Gathering legitimate reader reviews without violating policy • Organic sharing across WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok • Amazon Ads 101: Auto-targeting vs keyword targeting • Protecting ad spend profit margins.
Lesson 10 ⏱️ 15–25 mins

Build Your KDP Business: More Books, Series and Long-Term Growth

Learn how to repeat the process, build related books, create series, improve old books, and build a larger KDP catalog over time.

Topics Covered: The compounding catalog effect • Creating thematic book series • Revamping old covers and descriptions • The 90-Day Publisher Execution Blueprint (Month 1: Launch, Month 2: Scale, Month 3: Series).
