Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How we protect student data and privacy.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or enroll in Amazon KDP Simplified, we collect the necessary personal data required to create your course account and track your learning progress:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Encrypted authentication credentials managed securely via Supabase Auth.
  • Your course enrollment status and completed lesson progress.
  • Transaction confirmation records received from our payment platform (Selar).

2. How We Store and Protect Your Data

We do not store passwords in plaintext. All user authentication and database operations are managed using PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) on Supabase. This ensures students can only read and update their own authorized profile and progress records.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We partner with industry-standard third-party infrastructure to deliver the course:

  • Selar: Handles secure payment processing (card, bank transfer, mobile money). We do not store financial card numbers.
  • Wistia: Streams video lessons securely with high-speed global CDN delivery.
  • Supabase: Provides enterprise-grade encrypted cloud database and authentication services.
  • Netlify: Hosts the front-end application with HTTPS encryption.

4. No Sale of Personal Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal contact information to third-party advertisers or marketers.

5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request access to the data associated with your profile or request complete deletion of your account by contacting us at support@bookformatterpro.com.
