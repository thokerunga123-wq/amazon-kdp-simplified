Admin Protocol & Single-Device Enforcement

  • Generating Credentials: Click "Generate Student Credentials" above when a student pays on Selar. Enter their email, click generate, and copy the formatted WhatsApp notification message.
  • Single-Device Lock: The first device (laptop/PC) the student logs in with will automatically bind to their profile. Subsequent logins from any other device or computer will be blocked.
  • Resetting Device: If a student legitimately gets a new laptop, click "Reset Device" next to their record to clear their bound device ID.
