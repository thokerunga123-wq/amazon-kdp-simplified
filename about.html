Where My Journey Began

A few years ago, I was working in the medical field here in Uganda. Like many people, I kept hearing about online business opportunities and how authors were generating royalties on Amazon.

At first, I didn't believe it was realistic for someone in Africa. I believed Amazon publishing was only open to native English speakers living in the United States, professional writers with English degrees, or people with thousands of dollars in investment capital.

I did not have large capital. I was not an established author. But I decided to put doubts aside and try.

The Real Struggles of a Beginner

The first few months were tough. I made mistakes that almost made me quit:

  • I didn't understand market demand and wrote on topics nobody searched for.
  • My early covers looked amateur and received zero clicks.
  • I struggled with formatting margins, getting rejected repeatedly by Amazon Print.
  • I had no clue how to legally set up a US bank account to receive dollar royalties in Uganda.

I watched countless confusing YouTube tutorials, many of which gave conflicting advice or recommended risky shortcuts that violated Amazon rules.

"I realized that to succeed as an African publisher, I needed a clean, repeatable, 100% compliant process that eliminated guesswork."

Cracking the Code and Building Software

Once I focused on proper market validation and structured writing workflows, the breakthrough came: my books started selling, and I received my first royalty deposits directly in US Dollars.

Formatting was still the slowest, most frustrating bottleneck. That frustration drove me to develop Book Formatter Pro — an automated tool engineered to take manuscripts and format them into clean, Amazon-ready print and digital books in minutes.

Why I Created This Course

I created Amazon KDP Simplified because I know exactly how overwhelming the process feels when starting out in Uganda and across Africa.

I don't teach shortcuts or fake schemes. I teach honest, high-standard digital publishing that empowers students, employees, and small business owners to build an asset they own.
